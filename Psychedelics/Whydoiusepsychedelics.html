Myth 4 - Cannabis kills neurons
Based on a study conducted by the University of Louisville, long-term and heavy cannabis use does not kill brain cells. The active compounds found in the plant are potent antioxidants, meaning they protect DNA from damage caused by environmental and biological toxins. Research has shown that these antioxidant properties are neuroprotective, that is, they fight brain cell damage. What’s more, cannabis compounds are on the list of few substances that promote the growth of new brain cells.
